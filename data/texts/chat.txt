User: Hey, I'm planning a weekend trip with two friends and we can't agree on where to go. One wants the mountains, one wants the beach, and I just want somewhere with good food. Any ideas on how to settle it?
Assistant: A fun way to settle it is to look for a place that has a bit of all three. Many coastal towns sit close to hills, so you could spend one morning hiking, one afternoon at the beach, and every evening eating somewhere local. If that doesn't work, try a simple vote where each person ranks the options, and pick the one with the best average. Whatever you choose, book the restaurant early, because good places fill up on weekends.
User: That's a nice idea. How much should we budget for three days?
Assistant: It depends a lot on the country, but a rough plan is to split the money into travel, a place to sleep, food, and a small extra for activities. Sharing one apartment between three people is usually cheaper than three hotel rooms.
